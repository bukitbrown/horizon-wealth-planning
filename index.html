    Singapore money rules worth knowing early.

    Your CPF can help you buy your first home.

    Ordinary Account savings can go towards an HDB flat or private home, for the down payment and the monthly loan, within limits. Work out the cash you need now, not when the keys are near.

    Know what your health cover actually covers.

    MediShield Life covers every Singapore Citizen and PR for life. Integrated Shield Plans add higher ward and private hospital options on top.

    The first S$20,000 of taxable income is tax-free.

    For tax residents, the first S$20,000 of chargeable income is taxed at 0%, and CPF contributions and reliefs lower it further.

    CPF LIFE pays you a monthly income from 65.

    Your payout depends on your Retirement Account savings, so what you build in your twenties shapes the lifestyle you can afford at 65 and beyond. You can choose to defer payouts up to age 70.

    What we help you do with your pay.

    See exactly where your salary leaks.

    S$5 a day on drinks and delivery fees is S$150 a month, or S$1,800 a year. We find yours and decide what stays.

    Build a six-month safety net first.

    If your essentials cost S$2,000 a month, the target is S$12,000. Saving S$500 a month gets you there in 24 months.

    Check your hospital and life cover.

    Term life and critical illness plans cost less the younger and healthier you are when you start. We compare options and explain the fine print.

    Put your CPF to work, not to sleep.

    Your Ordinary Account earns up to 2.5% a year, Special and MediSave up to 4%, with 1% extra on your first S$60,000 combined (up to S$20,000 from OA). Cash top-ups to your own SA can also earn up to S$8,000 of tax relief.

    Invest small, steady and diversified.

    S$300 a month at an assumed 4% a year grows to about S$355,000 by 65 if you start at 25. We help you choose low-cost, diversified options that match your risk comfort.

    A note for your first payslip.

    Nobody teaches you what to do with your first payslip.

    You worked for years to land this job. Then the salary arrives, CPF takes its share, rent and meals and the sale on your phone take theirs, and by the 25th you wonder where it all went.

    That feeling is not a character flaw. It is what happens when money has no plan. The good news is that the earliest years are the cheapest to fix: a small habit at 24 does more work than a big effort at 34, because time does the heavy lifting.

    You do not need to be rich, or to understand every product. You need to know your numbers, protect yourself from the big surprises, and give every dollar a job. That is all we ask of you on the first call. We will do the rest with you.
